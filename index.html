OUR STORY

Made with love,
shared with you.

Little Bliss Cakes and Pastry Shop began with April’s passion for baking while she was a senior high school student. Through her family’s first business venture, Knocktime, she continued building her skills and growing her recipe collection.

After years of learning, wins, and challenges, Little Bliss began as a home-based online business and later opened its first local cake shop in Bato. Today, the heart of Little Bliss remains the same: quality products, thoughtful service, and meaningful experiences.
